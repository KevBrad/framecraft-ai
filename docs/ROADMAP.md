# Limits and proposed roadmap

## Current limits

Heuristic selection can miss context. Noisy ASR may produce garbled quotes or incorrect names. Word times are model estimates; a 150 ms forced-alignment target has not been independently established. Scene/face tracking is not active-speaker identity or visual-action understanding. Stacked layouts, intelligent punch-ins, transcript-based footage removal, generative B-roll and 4K controls are absent. Live Drive account authorization remains unverified. YouTube metadata is not general media ingestion. The local app has no account/tenant boundary or verified hosted operating model.

## Evidence gates for future work

1. **Selection and timing:** independently labelled source intervals, word/timing annotations, difficult-speech evaluation and failure-case review.
2. **Creator workflow:** proposed 5–10 creator trials measuring clips kept, editing effort, usability and willingness to pay. These are planned trials, not existing users.
3. **Hosted operation:** security/tenancy review, processing-cost measurements, reliable queues and storage controls before a limited cloud pilot.

Subscriptions, usage tiers, team editing and licensed integrations are business/product hypotheses. No dates, revenue projections, fundraising amount or market traction is claimed.
